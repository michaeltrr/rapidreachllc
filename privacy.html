Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you contact Rapid Reach LLC through this website — by email, contact form, or a scheduled call — we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, company or team name, and anything you tell us about your business.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, schedule and prepare for discovery calls, and, if you become a client, to deliver and manage our sales outsourcing services. We do not sell your information to third parties.

How we store it

Information you send us is stored in the tools we use to run the business — email, scheduling, and, for active clients, the CRM your team already uses. Access is limited to the people at Rapid Reach who need it to do their work.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing michaelt@rapidreachllc.biz.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to michaelt@rapidreachllc.biz.
